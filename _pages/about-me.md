I'm **Dr Raahat Manrai, PhD** — an online counsellor and the founder of **North Star Counselling**, based in Edinburgh. I offer confidential, compassionate support to clients worldwide through secure video sessions.

My background as a mental health researcher at the University of Edinburgh informs how I practise: warm, evidence-aware, and always centred on the person in front of me.

## Qualifications

- PhD in Clinical Psychology, University of Edinburgh (2020–2023)
- MSc (Hons) in Children and Young People's Mental Health and Psychological Practice, University of Edinburgh (2018–2019)
- BA (Hons) in Psychology, Panjab University, India (BPS accredited)
- Associate Fellow, Higher Education Academy (Advance HE)

## Research highlights

- Postdoctoral Research Associate, University of Edinburgh (Division of Psychiatry)
- **AMBIENT-BD** — Wellcome Trust study on sleep and circadian rhythms in bipolar disorder
- **SubSleep** — Principal Investigator, Edinburgh Mental Health Network seed-funded project
- Co-founder, **ConveRACEions** — improving racial equality in research

For a full list of publications, see my [publications](/publications/) page.

## Practice and teaching

- Youth worker, Edinburgh City Youth Cafe (6VT), since 2021 — supporting care-experienced young people
- Prior after-school playwork with children aged 5–12, including neurodivergent children
- MSc supervision and postgraduate tutoring at the University of Edinburgh

## Languages

English (fluent), Hindi (native), French (intermediate)
