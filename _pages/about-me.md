I'm **Dr Raahat Manrai, PhD** — an online counsellor offering confidential, compassionate support to clients worldwide. I founded **Turning Point Counselling** to make professional mental health support accessible, warm, and free from the barriers of distance or waiting rooms.

## Background and qualifications

I hold a PhD and bring both academic rigour and human-centred care to my practice. My background spans research and teaching, which informs a thoughtful, evidence-aware approach to counselling — always grounded in what matters most: you, your story, and your wellbeing.

For a full academic profile, see my [CV](/cv/) and [Publications](/publications/).

## My approach

I believe counselling works best when you feel safe, respected, and genuinely heard. My style is:

- **Warm and non-judgemental** — you can share at your own pace, without pressure to perform or explain
- **Collaborative** — we work together to understand what's happening and what might help
- **Practical** — sessions are tailored to your situation, not a one-size-fits-all formula
- **Confidential** — what you share stays between us, within the limits of professional practice

## Areas of focus

I work with adults navigating:

- Stress, anxiety, and worry
- Life transitions and major changes
- Relationship difficulties
- Low mood and emotional exhaustion
- Identity, belonging, and self-understanding
- Work–life balance and burnout
