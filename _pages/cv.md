## Profile

Mental health researcher with expertise in qualitative and participatory research methods, particularly co-production with marginalized populations. Research spans developmental trauma in care-experienced youth to sleep and circadian health in bipolar disorder. Specialized in longitudinal data collection using digital methods and stakeholder engagement.

## Research Experience

Responsibilities include collecting longitudinal data using digital technology (wearables and nearables), conducting participant recruitment and data collection, liaising with stakeholders, conducting co-production with people with bipolar disorder, and facilitating Lived Experience Advisory Panel meetings.

**Principal Investigator (SubSleep)**

Edinburgh Mental Health Network ECR Seed Funded project examining subjective experiences of sleep in people with bipolar disorder using semi-structured interviews, photovoice, and participatory research methods.

University of Edinburgh · January 2020 – December 2023

Conceptualised developmental trauma exposure in care-experienced young people using participatory action research and qualitative methods. Created 'Best Practice Framework' with adult stakeholders through co-production. IPT-AST training by Anna Freud Centre.

**Research Assistant**

Research assistant on neuro-trauma training project for residential care staff. Responsibilities included data collection, participant liaison, and facilitating focus groups.
