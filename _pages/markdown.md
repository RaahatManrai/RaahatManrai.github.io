## Session fees

Counselling sessions are **50 minutes** in length. Current fees are available on request — please [contact me](mailto:{{ site.author.email }}) or proceed to [book your session](/book-your-session/) for the latest rate.

> *This section will be updated with your confirmed fee. If you have a preferred rate, please share it and I will add it here.*

## What is included

Each session includes:

- A full **50-minute** one-to-one counselling session
- A secure, confidential online video call
- A safe space to explore what you're going through

## Payment

- Payment details will be confirmed at the time of booking
- Please ensure payment is completed according to the terms agreed when you book

## Cancellation policy

- Please provide at least **24 hours' notice** if you need to cancel or reschedule
- Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice may be charged in full
- If I need to cancel, I will offer an alternative time or a full refund

## Concessions

If cost is a barrier for you, please get in touch. I may be able to offer a limited number of reduced-rate sessions depending on availability.

## Questions?

If anything is unclear, email me at [{{ site.author.email }}](mailto:{{ site.author.email }}) before booking. I'm happy to answer any questions about fees, sessions, or how counselling works.
