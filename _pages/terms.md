## Important notice

**This website and counselling service are not for emergencies.** If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, please contact your local emergency services or a crisis helpline in your country.

## Counselling services

North Star Counselling provides online counselling sessions conducted via secure video call. By booking a session, you agree to the terms outlined on this page.

### Sessions

- Each session is **50 minutes** in duration
- Sessions are held online via video call
- You are responsible for ensuring a private, uninterrupted space and a stable internet connection
- Sessions are available to clients worldwide; please select your correct time zone when booking

### Confidentiality

What you share in counselling is treated as confidential, with the following exceptions required by professional practice:

- If there is a serious risk of harm to yourself or others
- If child or vulnerable adult safeguarding concerns arise
- If disclosure is required by law

I will discuss any limits to confidentiality with you at the start of our work together.

### Cancellations and rescheduling

- Please provide at least **24 hours' notice** to cancel or reschedule a session
- Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice may be charged in full
- If I need to cancel, I will offer an alternative time or a full refund

### Payment

Fees are as stated in the [Fee Guide](/fee-guide/) or as agreed at the time of booking. Payment terms will be confirmed when you book.
