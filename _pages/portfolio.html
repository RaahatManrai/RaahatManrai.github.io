--- layout: single title: "Session Structure" permalink: /session-structure/ author_profile: false --- Each session is **50 minutes** and held entirely online, so you can join from the comfort of your own home — wherever you are in the world. ## What to expect - A safe, confidential space to talk openly - A warm, non-judgemental approach - Practical support tailored to what you're going through - A pace that respects where you are — no pressure to have everything figured out ## How it works 1. **Book** — Choose a slot using the [Book your session](/book-your-session/) page. Please select your correct time zone when booking. 2. **Connect** — Join via a secure video call link sent to your email. Find a private, comfortable space with a stable internet connection. 3. **Begin** — We start where you are, at your own pace. There's no script — just a conversation focused on what matters to you. ## Before your first session - Choose a quiet, private location where you won't be interrupted - Test your camera, microphone, and internet connection beforehand - Have a glass of water nearby — small comforts help you settle in - You don't need to prepare anything specific; come as you are ## After your session There's no homework unless we agree it would be helpful. Some people like to take a few minutes to reflect quietly after a session. If questions arise between sessions, you're welcome to email me. If you have any questions before booking, feel free to [get in touch](mailto:{{ site.author.email }}).
